## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** This is a Halo page, not a leftover B2B template. Full-bleed sun, Gabarito, ink outlines, sticker shadows, a floating pill SiteHeader, and a real Windows desktop mock in DroppyMock. The visual world is authored. What is not authored is the argument. The first viewport is a generic “headline + dual CTA + cropped device” stack; SiteHeader is a six-item product sitemap; StationBoard is a ten-lane catalog; ChangelogTeaser reads like an engineering diary; SiteFooter dumps fifteen feature links. The living mascot the layout contract promises in one viewport is `z-0` behind the monitor and effectively invisible at the fold. Specificity is in the paint. The story still behaves like a feature-directory SaaS site wearing a sunny sticker.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI detector on 19 homepage markup files returned **0 findings** (exit 0, JSON `[]`). CSS-only files (`globals.css`, `dmk.css`) were excluded per critique protocol. Patterns that would match bounce-easing / layout-transition if CSS had been scanned: Hero GSAP `back.out(1.6)` on the mascot peek; NotchDemo `transition-[width,height,border-radius]`; Droppy `dmk.css` overshoot cubics and width/height transitions. Those are expected mock physics, not slop. Detector and design review **do not overlap** on the P0/P1 issues — download contradiction, fold composition, and catalog IA are invisible to the rule engine.

**Visual overlays:** No reliable user-visible overlay is available. Fallback signal: `no user-visible overlay; CLI detector + headless screenshots`. Live-server injection was skipped (no harness browser mutation/present API). Headless Edge captures were taken at 1440×900 and 390×844.

## Overall Impression

The sticker-shop world is real and distinctive. The persuasion path is not. Halo’s homepage currently sells a download it cannot fulfill, hides the product it claims to demo, and then asks the visitor to shop a ten-station catalog of album art. The single biggest opportunity: one honest conversion status, and the expanded island plus pokeable face in the first 900px.

## What's Working

1. **PrinciplesBand + Faq** — the only stretch that sounds like Halo for Windows power users: non-activating window, not always-listening, local app-data, GSMTC, Reduce Motion in Settings. Honest, specific, calm.
2. **Visual world in chrome** — SiteHeader pill, Gabarito display, 2.5px ink, sun fields. This is not another indigo SaaS landing.
3. **DroppyMock when it actually opens** — Kill Bill / SZA island on a Windows 11 desktop with a real taskbar is more product-led than any screenshot tile. That is the demo the first viewport should have been.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Primary action is a bait-and-switch
**What:** Hero `Button href="/download"`, SiteHeader “Download”, and SiteFooter “Download for Windows” are live. CtaSection renders the same label as a disabled `span`. Destination `/download` is another disabled brick. FeaturePageView keeps a live Download as well.
**Why it matters:** The page’s only success action cannot be completed, and the chrome disagrees with itself. Jordan clicks Download, lands on coming soon. Riley notices the header still sells the thing the closing panel disabled.
**Fix:** One status, everywhere. Until a Store/MSIX URL exists, header/hero/footer/CTA all say **Coming soon** (or **Notify me**) and point at one capture or `/changelog`. Never disable one Download while another still looks armed.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

### [P1] First viewport does not prove Halo
**What:** At 1440×900 the monitor is a gray sliver; the notch is a thin pill; Mascot is `hidden lg:block`, `z-0`, parked behind `data-hero-device`. “Meet the mascot” sells a character who is not on stage. Yellow primary on yellow field loses to the white secondary. Caption “Interactive demo · Click or tap the black pill” sits under the monitor, after the fold. Mobile stacks two CTAs above a cropped gray desktop with “Start demo” covering the notch.
**Why it matters:** The thesis is “mascot is the demo.” The fold shows a coming-soon chip and a cropped wallpaper.
**Fix:** Put the expanded island + pokeable face in the first 900px. Make the primary a high-contrast control (cloud fill on sun, or ink fill). Kill “Start demo” as a desktop ghost button; bind the tour to the pill.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`

### [P1] StationBoard is a catalog, not a proof
**What:** Ten always-visible tabs. Default Media panel is full-bleed TURBO anime, not notch chrome. Copy: “The pill you just clicked is the whole product.” On 390px the chips wrap to three rows and the video still isn’t Halo. Fixed SiteHeader overlaps “One notch. Every station.”
**Why it matters:** Jordan cannot form a 3-station model of the product. The proof of the island is replaced by album art.
**Fix:** Four stations max on the homepage (Media, Files, Calendar, AI). Show the island in the video, not the album art. Move the rest behind **See all features**. Add scroll-margin so the pill never eats H2s.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P1] Demo honesty for a native-Windows claim
**What:** `droppy-mock.html` / `droppy-mock.js` widgets include Pomodoro, TermiNotch, Weather, Thunderstorm, Lyrics. Calendar agenda is someone’s July: `test taak`, `Brunch bij mama`, `Padel met Bram`. PRODUCT.md forbids claiming Pomodoro as a full product.
**Why it matters:** The Windows power user is here to check “is this real, local, not Electron, not a Mac clone with toy widgets.” This mock answers “maybe a web toy.”
**Fix:** Strip non-product modules. Seed calendar/media with Halo-plausible Windows data. If it isn’t in the Win11 app, it does not appear in the island.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

### [P2] Chrome fights the page
**What:** Fixed SiteHeader overlaps the Media card and “One notch” column. Mobile menu restacks Logo + close on top of the already-open header. SiteFooter 15-link grid is a sitemap, not a close. Nav is six product destinations plus Download.
**Why it matters:** Casey never “gets it,” and they cannot take it with them. Riley opens the mobile menu and gets two X buttons.
**Fix:** 3–4 nav items. One close control in the sheet. Footer: Download/Coming soon, Privacy, Updates, Features.
**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

## Emotional journey

- **Peak (intended):** click the living notch / poke Cloudee. **Peak (actual):** PrinciplesBand — “Built like Windows software, not a browser tab.” The demo peak is below the fold and easy to miss.
- **Valley:** StationBoard (TURBO album-art video, ten chips, “pill you just clicked”) then ChangelogTeaser (0.10.0 coordinator bullets + the same TURBO frame).
- **End:** CtaSection washed-out disabled Download beside a crisp **Watch it evolve**; SiteFooter immediately offers a live Download. Peak-end is an apology, then a contradiction.
- **High-stakes reassurance:** Copy is good — native, local-first, assistant off, models removable. Control states lie. Jordan cannot leave with a file, a Store page, or an email catch.

Reduced motion: GSAP hero, marquee, Reveal, station transitions, and FeatureVideo autoplay are skippable. FAQ chevron, header menu, mascot poke, and Droppy morphs are not.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer):** Does not meet Cloudee in Hero. Reads seven verbs, clicks **Download for Windows**, lands on coming soon. StationBoard says they clicked a pill they never saw. **Meet the mascot** is a detour, not a proof.

**Riley (Stress Tester):** Clicks header Download (works), closing Download (dead), footer Download (works). Clicks `#dmk-notch` and **Start demo** — two tutors. Tabs through ten stations. Opens mobile menu and gets two X buttons. Finds Pomodoro in the mock after the page swore local-first Windows software.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile):** First screen: stacked CTAs, no Download in the pill, a cropped gray desktop, **Start demo** in the way of the notch. Stations: ten wrapping chips + TURBO still. Footer is a phone-book. They never “get it,” and they cannot take it with them.

**Windows power user (project-specific):** Wants native HWND, not Electron, not a widget toy. Gets a mono line “WINDOWS 11 · NATIVE · LOCAL-FIRST” and a web Droppy with Mac-island muscle memory and forbidden modules. Real proof is buried in FAQ item 1 and PrinciplesBand. ChangelogTeaser “Live Activity coordinator” does not answer “can I run this on this PC this week?”

## Questions to Consider

1. If nobody can download Halo, why is **Download** the loudest word in the header, the hero, the close, *and* the footer?
2. If the mascot is the product, why is Cloudee `z-0` behind a gray monitor while a white button has to take you to `/mascot` to meet it?
3. Would you trust a “native Windows notch” whose interactive proof includes Pomodoro and `Brunch bij mama`?
4. What is the first viewport *for* — get the build, believe the island, or fall in love with the face? It currently attempts all three and lands none.
5. Why should a Dynamic Island veteran scroll past ten station chips to reach the only sentence that matters: it is not a browser tab?
